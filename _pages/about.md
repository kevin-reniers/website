Hi, I'm Kevin, a cognitive neuroscientist, working as a PhD candidate in the Learning and Motivation lab (Donders Centre for Cognition) and the Predictive Brain lab (Donders Centre for Cognitive Neuroimaging) at the Radboud University in Nijmegen, the Netherlands. <br/>
My main field of interest is educational neuroscience. Using both behavioural and neuroimaging methods, I try to understand how the human brain is motivated to learn and explore in healthy adults and children -- in the lab and in the classroom. <br> 
Additionally, I focus on how we can use the findings from neuroscience to create learning environments that foster curiosity, growth and a life-long love for learning. Therefore, I try to bridge the gap between fundamental neuroscience and educational practice. One part of this is that I am studying to become a licensed high school physics teacher. Because teaching is incredibly fun, and it keeps you grounded! <br/>
I strive to do science in the most open and transparent way, and as much as possible in collaboration with a team of colleagues (#teamscience!). I also try to translate my work in 'normal-speak': publishing and speaking in easier, less formal language (as well as in Dutch) for non-neuroscientists to learn from what we do! <br/>
Interested in what I do? Feel free to send me an email or DM me on LinkedIn to have a quick chat!
