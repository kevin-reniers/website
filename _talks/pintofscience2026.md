**Why are we so curious?** <br>
Why can humans be so curious? Why do we always want to know new things: from watching more TikTok videos to learning quantum mechanics. We’ll go on a neuroscientific trip through our evolving understanding of curiosity, from past, to current research, and eventually the future: how can curiosity reshape education?
